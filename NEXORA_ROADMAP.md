**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Version:** 2.0

## 1. Architectural Vision

**NEIMAN** is an AI-native Operating System for intelligent organizations. It empowers teams to define operational workflows, assemble multi-agent departments, delegate complex decisions to heterogeneous AI councils (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI), and maintain complete governance and observability over all automated actions.

## 3. Next Implementation Priorities

### Phase 11 — Organizational Performance
- Multidimensional KPI metrics: cycle time, failure rate, rework, human intervention frequency.
- Expected vs. actual outcome evaluation across agents, departments, and models.

### Phase 12 — Evolution Engine
- Controlled adaptation pipeline: `OBSERVE` $\to$ `DIAGNOSE` $\to$ `PROPOSE` $\to$ `SIMULATE` $\to$ `EVALUATE` $\to$ `VALIDATE` $\to$ `APPROVE` $\to$ `DEPLOY` $\to$ `MONITOR`.
- Immutable snapshots and rollback capabilities for organizational configurations.

### Phase 13 — Simulation Lab
- Sandbox testing of organizational configurations (varying agent counts, model routing, and budget limits).

### Phase 14 — Natural Language "Build My Company"
- Conversational onboarding generating complete organizational structures ready for human approval and instantiation.

### Phase 15 — Multi-Provider Resilience & Circuit Breakers
- Real-time provider health monitors, dynamic fallbacks, and token ceiling enforcement.

### Phase 16 — Enterprise Security Hardening
- Prompt injection defenses, tool execution sandboxes, and least-privilege boundary verification.

### Phase 17 — Automated Self-Diagnostics
- End-to-end integration health validation and auto-remediation checks.
